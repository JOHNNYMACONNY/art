## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VERDICT

**VERDICT: ALL 4 MARKETING & DESIGN CLAIMS ARE EMPIRICALLY FALSIFIED.**

As an adversarial mobile playtester evaluating Godot scene `scrap_test_block.tscn` on iPhone 15 Pro, the current implementation fails to deliver an intuitive, clear, tense, or earned gameplay experience. While the underlying code passes synthetic assertion suites (`--run-v6-assertions`), human touch interaction, visual affordances, AI pathing, and collision safety logic contain critical game-breaking flaws (S0 severity), severe control friction, and design oversights that would cause a real mobile player to drop the game within the first 45 seconds.

| Claim Tested | Status | Critical Failure Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Controls are intuitive"** | **FALSIFIED** | Dynamic joystick center lacks re-centering, swipe gestures use unaccumulated single-frame deltas (`/200.0`), dial requires linear horizontal drag despite circular visual cues, and overshoots trigger harsh 2x dwell decay. |
| **"The objective is clear"** | **FALSIFIED** | Cold spawn at `(0, 0, 10)` faces an unpowered terminal with zero cable affordance or objective markers. Players hit dead ends and experience a "broken button" impression. |
| **"The chase is tense"** | **FALSIFIED** | Pursuer (`11.0 m/s`) is 21.4% slower than Courier Bike (`14.0 m/s`). Straightaway driving breaks pursuit at a tiny `18.0m` range, bypassing the route switch entirely. Pursuer AI overshoots foot turns, enabling infinite foot circles without interception. |
| **"The route switch feels earned"** | **FALSIFIED** | **CRITICAL (S0)**: Barrier collision enablement is infinitely delayed when occupied (`_try_enable_collision`), causing pursuers to phase through solid metal arms like ghosts. Gate interaction window is only 420ms at max speed with a 0.7s activation lag. |

## 3. ADVERSARIAL FINDINGS & FALSIFICATION LOGS

### 3.1 CLAIM 1 FALSIFICATION: "Controls Are Intuitive"

#### [FINDING-C1.1] Dynamic Touch Joystick Center Lacks Dynamic Drift, Creating Reversal Lag
- **TAXONOMY**: Controls / Touch Input Responsiveness
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**: 
  In [`touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L186-L197), touch start initializes `_joystick_center_pos = pos` on initial down-touch. When dragging past `max_joystick_radius = 80.0px`, `_current_joystick_vec` clamps to length 1.0, but `_joystick_center_pos` remains static. On an iPhone 15 Pro, a full thumb drag moves 150–220px across the glass. Reversing movement direction requires dragging the thumb backward over 70–140px of dead space before `delta_pos` crosses back through center.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"Why is my character frozen when I pull back? I'm sliding my thumb left to turn, but the bike keeps driving straight into the wall until I swipe halfway across the screen!"*
- **INFERENCE**: Static joystick base positioning without anchor follow causes massive deadzone hysteresis on direction changes.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Implement dynamic joystick base repositioning when thumb displacement exceeds `max_joystick_radius`: `_joystick_center_pos = pos - (delta_pos.normalized() * max_joystick_radius)`.

#### [FINDING-C1.2] Single-Frame Unaccumulated Peel Drag Velocity Scaling
- **TAXONOMY**: Controls / Gesture Recognition Mechanics
- **SEVERITY**: **S1 (Severe Usability Defect)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L179-L181), peel progress evaluates `var progress: float = clampf(drag_ev.relative.y / 200.0, 0.0, 1.0)`. In [`corroded_panel.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/corroded_panel.gd#L90-L100), `progress_peel(amount)` directly sets `panel_mesh.rotation.x = lerp(..., amount)` using this instantaneous single-frame delta. Because relative motion per frame at 60 FPS is typically 5–25px, `amount` evaluates to `0.025 - 0.125`. Unless the user violently swipes at an unphysical velocity exceeding 170px/frame (10,200 px/sec), `amount` never hits the `0.85` threshold, and the panel rubber-bands shut on the next frame.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"I'm dragging down on the panel over and over, but it just twitches slightly and snaps shut! I have to slash my screen violently like a crazy person to get it to stay open!"*
- **INFERENCE**: Mixing instantaneous frame delta relative distance with state transition thresholds instead of accumulating total drag distance causes gesture failure.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Accumulate drag distance across frames: `_peel_accumulated += drag_ev.relative.y / total_drag_length`, and pass `_peel_accumulated` to `progress_peel()`.

#### [FINDING-C1.3] Dial Overshooting & Linear Drag / Circular Visual Mismatch
- **TAXONOMY**: Controls / UI Feedback Disconnect
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L182-L184), signal tuning listens to linear horizontal drag: `var delta_freq: float = drag_ev.relative.x / 300.0`. However, [`touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L140) displays the hint `"[ ROTATE DIAL TO LOCK FREQUENCY ]"`. When players attempt to rotate their thumb circularly over the 3D dial mesh, horizontal `relative.x` oscillates positive and negative, sending `current_frequency` back and forth. Furthermore, in [`signal_tuner.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_tuner.gd#L73), overshooting the ±0.05 window penalizes dwell timer at 2x rate (`_dwell_timer - delta * 2.0`), wiping out lock progress instantly.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"The game tells me to rotate the dial, but spinning my finger makes the meter bounce around like crazy. When I finally hit the green zone, if I twitch for a split millisecond, the lock timer resets completely."*
- **INFERENCE**: Mismatch between rotational UX prompt and 1D horizontal touch parsing, combined with harsh dwell decay, creates unnecessary tuning frustration.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Calculate true rotational touch angle relative to dial center (`atan2(touch.y - center.y, touch.x - center.x)`), add notch magnetic snapping around `target_frequency`, and reduce overshoot decay to `1.0 * delta`.

### 3.2 CLAIM 2 FALSIFICATION: "The Objective Is Clear"

#### [FINDING-O2.1] Cold Spawn Directional Blindness & Missing Environmental Guidance
- **TAXONOMY**: Design / Onboarding & Environmental Guidance
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  Player spawns at `Vector3(0, 0, 10.0)`. [`signal_tuner.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_tuner.gd#L30-L47) starts in `DORMANT` state when player is > 6.0m away (spawn is 13.5m away at `z = -3.5`). [`corroded_panel.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/corroded_panel.gd#L30) starts completely unpowered (`is_powered = false`). There are no objective indicators, no glowing power conduits from tuner to panel, and no camera orientation hints.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"I spawned in a dark alley with a bike to my left and a glowing wall in front of me. I don't know where I'm supposed to go or what I'm trying to accomplish."*
- **INFERENCE**: Lack of initial environmental visual hierarchy or camera pull leaves the player wandering without direction.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Add a faint pulse lighting effect along `PowerConduit` leading toward `SignalTuner`, or trigger a 1-second camera pan to the active tuner node on scene start.

#### [FINDING-O2.2] Unpowered Terminal Dead-End & False "Broken Button" Perception
- **TAXONOMY**: UX / Interaction Feedback
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  If the player walks straight to `CorrodedPanel` prior to tuning the signal, pressing the Action Button produces zero audio feedback, zero visual response, and zero UI hint. In [`scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L286-L296), `_evaluate_target_selection()` ignores `CorrodedPanel` because `can_interact()` returns false when `is_powered == false`.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"I ran up to the rusty box, tapped the big action button three times, and nothing happened. I thought the button was broken."*
- **INFERENCE**: Suppressing interactive prompts completely on unpowered targets without displaying an "UNPOWERED - REQUIRES SIGNAL LOCK" feedback label confuses players.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Allow targeting of unpowered interactables, displaying a greyed-out prompt: `"[ UNPOWERED - LOCK SIGNAL FIRST ]"`.

### 3.3 CLAIM 3 FALSIFICATION: "The Chase Is Tense"

#### [FINDING-T3.1] Inverted Pursuit Speed Delta (Zero Threat Profile)
- **TAXONOMY**: Game Balance / Pursuit Mechanics
- **SEVERITY**: **S1 (Severe Design Flaw)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`courier_bike.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/vehicles/courier_bike.gd#L19), bike `max_speed = 14.0 m/s`. In [`pursuer_prototype.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/entities/pursuer_prototype.gd#L9), pursuer `max_speed = 11.0 m/s`. When pursuit triggers, holding GAS drives the bike down the straightaway at 14.0 m/s. The pursuer falls behind at a rate of 3.0 m/s, dropping out of threat range within seconds without requiring any evasive maneuvers.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"I just held down the gas button and drove straight. The police car couldn't even catch up to me. There was zero challenge or danger."*
- **INFERENCE**: Pursuer top speed is 21.4% slower than the player's primary escape vehicle, rendering pursuit completely trivial on straightaways.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Increase pursuer top speed to `16.5 m/s` (17.8% faster than bike), forcing the player to use environmental obstacles and route switching to break line-of-sight.

#### [FINDING-T3.2] Ultra-Narrow 18.0m Contact Broken Threshold Bypasses Route Switch
- **TAXONOMY**: Game Balance / Sequence Bypass
- **SEVERITY**: **S1 (Severe Design Flaw)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L179-L187), driving > 18.0m ahead of the pursuer starts `_contact_broken_timer`. After 3.0s (plus 1.0s decay), the game enters `EVADED`, terminating pursuit and showing the Replay screen. Because the bike naturally outruns the pursuer, a player can trigger evasion by simply driving forward for 4.5 seconds, completely bypassing the Signal Gate route switch mechanic!
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"Wait, the pursuit ended and I won? I didn't even activate the scrap gate or switch routes!"*
- **INFERENCE**: The evasion threshold distance is far too small (18.0m is barely outside camera view), allowing straightaway speed alone to defeat the core scene objective.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Increase `contact_broken` distance to `35.0m`, and require line-of-sight obstruction (or SignalGate closure) to trigger evasion decay.

#### [FINDING-T3.3] Pursuer Foot Steering Overshoot Exploit (Infinite Circle Stalling)
- **TAXONOMY**: AI / Physics Exploits
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Exploitation)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`pursuer_prototype.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/entities/pursuer_prototype.gd#L80-L93), pursuer steering uses `current_forward.slerp(desired_dir, 4.0 * delta)` with `intercept_distance = 1.5m` and continuous `_intercept_timer >= 0.35s`. If the player on foot (`move_speed = 8.5 m/s`) runs in tight circles around the pursuer, the pursuer's limited turning radius (`steering_speed = 4.0`) causes it to constantly overshoot. The pursuer briefly enters 1.5m range for 0.1s before overshooting to 2.2m, which resets `_intercept_timer` via `move_toward(..., 0.0, delta * 2.0)`.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"If I get caught on foot, I just run in a tiny circle around the pursuer. He keeps spinning in circles missing me forever."*
- **INFERENCE**: Rigid slerp turning radius without speed reduction during sharp turns, coupled with aggressive intercept timer decay, creates a soft-lock evasion exploit.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Implement braking during sharp turns in `pursuer_prototype.gd`, and lower intercept dwell requirement to `0.15s` or use instant contact collision checks.

### 3.4 CLAIM 4 FALSIFICATION: "The Route Switch Feels Earned"

#### [FINDING-R4.1] CRITICAL (S0): Ghost Sweep Area Occupancy Infinite Collision Delay
- **TAXONOMY**: Collision / Critical Game-Breaking Physics Bug
- **SEVERITY**: **S0 (Critical Failure - Game-Breaking)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`signal_gate_interactable.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_gate_interactable.gd#L70-L107), when `trigger_gate()` executes, `barrier_collision.disabled` is set to `true` while `barrier_pivot` rotates 90 degrees over 0.3s. Upon completion, `_try_enable_collision()` checks `is_sweep_occupied()`. `is_sweep_occupied()` checks if ANY `CharacterBody3D` is inside `SweepArea`. Because `PursuerPrototype` is a `CharacterBody3D` trailing closely behind the bike, if the pursuer is inside `SweepArea` when the barrier finishes swinging, `is_sweep_occupied()` returns `true`. `_try_enable_collision()` postpones enabling collision by 0.1s repeatedly. As a result, `barrier_collision.disabled` remains `TRUE` indefinitely while the pursuer is near the gate, allowing the pursuer to phase straight through the solid metal barrier arm as if it were a ghost!
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"I hit the route switch button, watched the heavy steel barrier arm slam shut, and then the police vehicle drove straight THROUGH the solid steel barrier like it was made of smoke! That is totally broken!"*
- **INFERENCE**: Misconfigured safety sweep check includes threat entities (`PursuerPrototype`) in the exclusion filter, preventing physical barrier collision from ever solidifying when a pursuer is close.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Update `is_sweep_occupied()` in [`signal_gate_interactable.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_gate_interactable.gd#L70) to exclude `PursuerPrototype` from safety delay, or apply hit impulse / destruction collision to pursuers caught in the sweep.

#### [FINDING-R4.2] Detour Waypoint U-Turn Teleportation
- **TAXONOMY**: AI / Visual Art Disconnect
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Visual Bug)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L241-L246), triggering the gate sets hardcoded detour waypoints: `[Vector3(2.5, 0.6, 8.0), Vector3(2.5, 0.6, 18.0), Vector3(-1.5, 0.6, 26.0)]`. If the pursuer has already driven past `z = 8.0` (e.g. at `z = 10.5`), assigning waypoint 0 at `z = 8.0` causes the pursuer to violently execute a 180-degree U-turn backwards up the track toward `z = 8.0` before resuming forward motion.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"When the gate closed, the pursuer abruptly flipped backward and started driving back toward the spawn point before turning around again. It looked like an AI glitch."*
- **INFERENCE**: Static hardcoded detour waypoints do not account for pursuer dynamic position along the track at the moment of activation.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Dynamically compute detour waypoints relative to pursuer current Z coordinate: `Vector3(2.5, 0.6, pursuer.global_position.z + 2.0)`.

#### [FINDING-R4.3] 420ms Fleeting Touch Window & 0.7s Gate Activation Latency
- **TAXONOMY**: Usability / Timing & Reaction Windows
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`signal_gate_interactable.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_gate_interactable.gd#L29), `interaction_radius = 6.0m`. At bike top speed (`14.0 m/s`), the bike crosses this 6.0m zone in **0.428 seconds** (25.7 frames). The `RouteSwitchButton` on the UI appears and disappears in less than half a second. Furthermore, after pressing the button, `activation_time = 0.4s` charge + `0.3s` barrier animation creates a **0.70 second** total mechanical delay before the barrier closes. By the time the gate shuts, the player's bike is already 9.8 meters past the gate.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"A button popped up on my screen for a split second while I was steering, and before I could move my right thumb to tap it, it vanished!"*
- **INFERENCE**: Inadequate interaction radius for high-speed vehicle approach creates an unmanageable reaction window for touch screen controls.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Increase `SignalGateInteractable` `interaction_radius` to `15.0 meters` for vehicle traversal (providing a 1.07s reaction window at top speed), and reduce gate activation charge time to `0.15 seconds`.

## 5. EXIT QUESTIONNAIRE (PLAYTESTER P01)

### Q1: Would you voluntarily play this game session again in its current state?
**ANSWER: NO.**
*Rationale*: The core vehicle chase lacks tension because the pursuer is slower than the bike, and the one cool mechanical moment (slamming a scrap gate shut) is ruined when the pursuer phase-shifts through the physical metal arm due to a collision check bug.

### Q2: What was the single most frustrating moment during your playtest?
**ANSWER**: Trying to peel the corroded panel with touch gestures. Because drag deltas aren't accumulated across frames, sliding my thumb down normally just made the panel twitch and snap shut. I had to violently swipe my finger across the screen like a weapon to get it to register.

### Q3: Did the touch controls feel natural for an iPhone 15 Pro?
**ANSWER: NO.**
The joystick center stays stuck where you first touch, so changing directions after a long swipe has a massive dead zone. The tuner tells you to "rotate" the dial, but spinning your thumb breaks the frequency tuning because it only measures straight left-to-right dragging.

### Q4: Did you feel motivated to switch routes using the Signal Gate?
**ANSWER: NO.**
First, the button only stays on screen for less than half a second while driving at full speed. Second, I realized I didn't even need to use the gate—if I just drove straight for 4 seconds, the game declared I "evaded" the pursuer anyway.
