### Focus Area 2: Peel Panel Gesture Hitboxes & Gesture Cancellation
- `OBSERVED`:
  - `0:27.4` - Player triggers `CorrodedPanel` interaction (`current_step = Step.PEELING`). `GestureOverlayPanel` appears.
  - `0:28.1 - 0:34.5` - Player attempts to swipe down with right thumb. `touch_controls.gd` computes `progress = clampf(drag_ev.relative.y / 200.0, 0.0, 1.0)`. Because `drag_ev.relative.y` is single-frame delta (e.g. 8px - 25px per frame), `progress` evaluates to 0.04 - 0.12 per frame, setting `panel_mesh.rotation.x = lerp(..., amount)`.
  - `0:30.2` - The metal panel violently stutters open 10 degrees and instantly snaps shut whenever swipe velocity drops below 170px/frame (85% threshold).
  - `0:32.8` - Player releases thumb (`touch_ev.pressed == false`). `touch_controls.gd` sets `_is_peeling = false`, but NEVER calls `corroded_panel.cancel_interaction()`. `corroded_panel.current_step` remains locked in `Step.PEELING`.
  - `0:34.0` - Player attempts to tap screen again to re-peel. `can_interact()` returns `false` (requires `Step.APPROACHED`), and `trigger_action()` returns `false`. Player is permanently soft-locked with `is_input_locked = true` and cannot move, peel, or proceed.
- `PLAYER_REACTION`:
  - *"The lid is fluttering like a broken shutter! I'm swiping down hard, but it snaps back shut!"*
  - *"Now my character is frozen in place! Tapping the screen does nothing! The game is completely stuck!"*
- `INFERENCE`:
  - `progress_peel` uses non-accumulated single-frame relative drag deltas (`relative.y / 200.0`) instead of cumulative displacement (`sum(relative.y)`). Furthermore, missing `cancel_interaction()` invocation on touch release leaves `CorrodedPanel` in `Step.PEELING`, producing a game-breaking soft-lock (S4 severity).
- `RECOMMENDATION`:
  - Store cumulative gesture displacement (`_peel_accumulated_y += relative.y`) to determine peel progress. Call `cancel_interaction()` on touch release if progress threshold is not met to gracefully reset panel state to `APPROACHED`.
