## Detailed Findings

### OBSERVED
1. **Clean Drive Baseline**: Driving straight without errors yields a gate press distance of `12.48m` and a gate slam distance of `10.64m`, leaving a minimum pursuer proximity of `10.41m`.
2. **Imperfect Turn / Wall Bump (1 Mistake)**: Dropping speed to `5.0 m/s` for 0.35s due to wall contact/steering clipping reduces gate press proximity to `7.95m` and gate slam proximity to `2.62m`. The pursuer reaches a minimum distance of `1.96m`, which remains above the `1.5m` interception threshold.
3. **Late Gate Notice / Accidental Brake (1 Mistake)**: Hitting BRAKE for 0.3s and delaying gate interaction by 0.35s drops speed to `4.18 m/s`. Gate press proximity drops to `5.04m` and gate slam proximity to `2.51m`. The pursuer reaches a minimum distance of `2.49m`, avoiding interception.
4. **Detour Backtracking Exploit**: Triggering SignalGate when the pursuer is already past the barrier origin (`z = 10.5m` vs `gate z = 12.0m`) causes the pursuer to acquire detour waypoint 0 at `z = 8.0m` behind it, resulting in a sudden 180° spin and backward speed of `-10.0 m/s`.

### INFERENCE
1. **Fairness Balance**: `max_speed = 15.5 m/s` (with `acceleration = 14.0 m/s²`) provides high visual threat and tension while maintaining a ~0.5m–1.0m mistake margin for single minor driving errors. The 0.75s disturbance alert delay + 0.35s bike mount time create a baseline 13.1m lead that absorbs one minor error.
2. **Recovery Viability**: Players can recover from 1 minor mistake (wall bump or late reaction) because the SignalGate interaction radius (`6.0m`) and gate slam delay (`0.4s` activation + `0.3s` arm swing) allow the barrier to drop before the pursuer can close the remaining ~2.0m gap.
3. **Detour Waypoint Routing Bug**: When detour path assignment occurs after the pursuer has passed waypoint 0 (`z = 8.0m`), the pursuer AI prioritizes strict sequential waypoint index ordering rather than skipping already-passed behind-the-barrier waypoints.

---

### RECOMMENDATION
1. **Keep Pursuer `max_speed = 15.5 m/s`**: The current speed tuning is fair, recoverable, and creates high counterplay satisfaction.
2. **WaypointsReroute Sanitization**: Modify `PursuerPrototype.set_detour_path()` to prune or skip detour waypoints that lie behind the pursuer's current position along the track axis (`global_position.z`), preventing backward turnaround exploits.
