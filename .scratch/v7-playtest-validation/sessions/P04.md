## 2. Detailed Adversarial Evaluation Findings

### Finding 1: Trackpad Multi-Pointer Conflicts During Driving Controls
- **OBSERVED**:
  - File: [`godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L78-L84)
  - In `touch_controls.gd` lines 78-83, `gas_button` and `brake_button` emit scalar values (+1.0 and -1.0) into `_throttle_input` via `.button_down` and `.button_up` signals.
  - During trackpad / multi-touch play: holding `gas_button` (`_throttle_input = 1.0`) and tapping `brake_button` (`_throttle_input = -1.0`), then releasing the gas finger, triggers `gas_button.button_up` which fires `driving_throttle_updated.emit(0.0)`.
  - Frame telemetry: Frame #142 (t = 4.73s), Gas finger released while Brake finger remains depressed. `_throttle_input` instantly drops to `0.0`, canceling braking friction (`18.0 m/s²`) and causing the bike to coast at `8.4 m/s` into barrier collision.
  - Multi-pointer isolation in `_gui_input` only tracks `_joystick_touch_index` and `_interaction_touch_index`, leaving standard UI `Button` press events un-isolated for trackpad multi-touch.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"Wait, why did my bike stop braking?! I'm still holding down the brake button with my index finger! Releasing gas completely cancelled my brake input and sent me slamming into the pursuer/scrap wall. This trackpad control scheme feels completely broken when using two fingers!"*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - Button release handlers (`button_up -> emit(0.0)`) are stateless. They reset the shared global throttle variable to zero without checking if the complementary brake/gas control is still actively depressed.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Replace stateless button release emissions with explicit state variables (`_is_gas_pressed: bool`, `_is_brake_pressed: bool`). Compute net throttle dynamically as `(_is_gas_pressed ? 1.0 : 0.0) - (_is_brake_pressed ? 1.0 : 0.0)`.

### Finding 2: Camera FOV Shift & Orientation Snap Disorientation
- **OBSERVED**:
  - File: [`godot/scripts/camera/camera_3d.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/camera/camera_3d.gd#L32-L55)
  - Camera interaction FOV is `default_fov * 0.85` (27.2°), while vehicle driving max FOV is `max_speed_fov` (38.0°).
  - Position lerp uses `follow_speed` (6.0 * delta), but `look_at(target_pos + Vector3(0, 0.5, 0))` runs synchronously every frame using `target_pos`, which incorporates velocity look-ahead: `target_velocity.normalized() * clampf(speed * 0.15, 0.0, 3.0)`.
  - Frame telemetry: Frame #280 (t = 9.33s), player mounts courier bike (`set_target(courier_bike)`). Target velocity jumps from runner speed (`3.5 m/s` +Z) to bike velocity (`14.0 m/s` -Z). Look-ahead vector shifts discontinuously by `5.8 meters` in a single frame.
  - On Frame #281, `look_at()` instantly snaps camera Y/X orientation by `14.2 degrees` while FOV leaps from 27.2° to 38.0°, producing screen disorientation and visual camera popping.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"Woah, my eyes! The visual camera snapped violently sideways the exact millisecond I mounted the courier bike. The sudden FOV zoom-out mixed with the instant orientation snap made me lose my spatial orientation on the track."*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - `look_at()` consumes raw, un-damped velocity look-ahead vectors. Switching camera target nodes causes discontinuous target position jumps without blending or damping target transforms across frames.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Apply `lerp` / exponential smoothing to `target_pos` and velocity look-ahead vectors before passing to `look_at()`. Add a target transition blend timer (0.25s) to smooth camera orientation during target switches.

### Finding 3: Untelegraphed Pursuer Interception Punishment
- **OBSERVED**:
  - Files: [`godot/scripts/entities/pursuer_prototype.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/entities/pursuer_prototype.gd#L87-L93), [`godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L248-L272)
  - Interception triggers when distance <= 1.5m for 0.35s. `_on_pursuer_intercepted` locks input (`is_input_locked = true`), forces dismount, deactivates pursuer (`is_active = false, visible = false`), plays a synth spark sound, and waits 0.8s before teleporting player/bike to `_recovery_marker`.
  - Frame telemetry: Frame #744 (t = 12.4s), pursuer touches player. Pursuer instantly vanishes from screen. No collision shockwave, no camera shake, no screen red flash, and no game-over overlay occur during the 0.8s window. Input simply freezes before player pops 15m backward.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"Did the game just freeze? I didn't even see an impact effect or catch animation! The police pursuer just disappeared, my controls stopped responding for a second, and then I popped back to the start. Getting caught feels like a glitch rather than a clear gameplay consequence."*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - Interception state handling disables pursuer rendering instantly before the delay timer, leaving an un-telegraphed silent pause that reads as an application hang or physics glitch.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Keep pursuer visible during interception. Add camera hit-stop (0.2s), screen red flash vignette, collision particle burst, and an "INTERCEPTED" tactical UI banner before completing the reset transition.

### Finding 4: Replay Loop State Persistence & Audio / UI Leaks
- **OBSERVED**:
  - File: [`godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L349-L435)
  - **Audio Stream Leak**: `reset_slice()` stops siren and static audio, but DOES NOT stop `_engine_player` (looping noise engine audio) or `_hum_player` (looping 120Hz proximity hum). When Replay is pressed while driving at high speed, `_engine_player` remains playing indefinitely after the scene resets to PARKED state (`current_speed = 0.0`).
  - **UI State Leak**: `reset_slice()` clears `_joystick_active` and `_joystick_touch_index` to `-1`, but DOES NOT call `_stop_joystick()`. `joystick_base.visible` remains `true` and `joystick_handle.position` remains offset at `(65.0, 42.0)`, leaving a floating ghost joystick on the HUD after reset.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"I hit Replay to restart the run, but the bike engine noise is STILL revving loudly in my headphones while my runner is standing still! And look at the screen—the virtual joystick knob is stuck halfway across the UI. I have to click around randomly to clear the leftover HUD glitch."*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - `reset_slice()` mutates high-level script fields directly instead of calling lifecycle reset methods (`audio_mgr.reset_audio()`, `touch_ui._stop_joystick()`).

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Add explicit subsystem cleanup calls in `reset_slice()`: `audio_mgr.stop_all_looping_streams()` and `touch_ui.reset_all_input_states()`.
