## 3. Adversarial Focus Area Evaluation & Empirical Findings

### Focus Area 1: Large Screen Reachability & Ergonomics (12.9" Display)

- **OBSERVED**:
  - Device: iPad Pro 12.9" in two-handed edge grip (thumbs positioned near lower screen corners at `x < 200px` and `x > 2532px`).
  - At `00:10.5`, `SignalTuner` interaction triggers `GestureOverlayPanel` with prompt `[ ROTATE DIAL TO LOCK FREQUENCY ]`.
  - In [`touch_controls.gd:178`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L178), tuning drag delta is computed as `delta_freq = drag_ev.relative.x / 300.0`. Touch index listener [`touch_controls.gd:157`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L157) binds `_interaction_touch_index` to ANY screen touch down when `gesture_panel.visible` is true.
  - To turn the dial, the player must lift right thumb from the right edge bezel and reach **140mm (5.5 inches)** into the center screen glass (`Vector2(1366, 1024)` in viewport coordinates).
  - While holding the 682g iPad Pro with left hand alone, right thumb drag produces diagonal drift (`relative.y = -18px`). This causes 4 overshooting oscillations between frequency `0.32` and `0.89` before landing within target tolerance `0.72 ± 0.05`.
  - At `00:27.5`, `CorrodedPanel` peel prompt `[ SWIPE DOWN TO PEEL PANEL ]` requires a vertical downward drag of `drag_ev.relative.y / 200.0`. Swiping down 200 points in screen center while balancing the tablet causes the player's palm to accidentally touch the edge bezel, firing redundant touch events.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"Why do I have to take my hand off the side of this massive iPad just to drag something in the middle of the screen? My wrist is straining just holding this thing with one hand!"*
  - *"The dial jumps all over the place! One tiny swipe sent it from 30% to 90%! I can't fine-tune this with one hand balancing a 13-inch slab."*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - The gesture overlay system treats 12.9" tablet screens identically to compact 5.8" smartphone screens.
  - Center-screen modal interaction overlays violate two-handed mobile/tablet grip mechanics. Drag scaling (`300.0` px divisor) does not scale with physical DPI or screen aspect ratio, creating excessive gain on large high-density touch surfaces.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Re-anchor interaction touch gestures (tuner dial and peel swipe) to dual-thumb lateral touch zones (left/right bottom quarters) rather than enforcing center-screen touch targets.
  - Dynamically scale gesture pixel sensitivity based on screen resolution (`get_viewport_rect().size`).

### Focus Area 2: High-Speed Driving & Oversteer / Joystick Radius Dropping

- **OBSERVED**:
  - In [`touch_controls.gd:22`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L22), `@export var max_joystick_radius: float = 80.0`. On a 2732x2048 display, 80 pixels represents **less than 12mm** of physical thumb travel.
  - In [`courier_bike.gd:55`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/vehicles/courier_bike.gd#L55) and [`touch_controls.gd:205`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L205): `rotate_y(-steering_angle * steering_speed * delta)` with `steering_speed = 2.5` rad/s and `max_speed = 14.0` m/s.
  - At `00:52.0`, while accelerating down the main scrap lane at `12.4 m/s`, player displaces left thumb by 45 pixels horizontally. Because 45px is > 50% of the 80px radius, `_current_joystick_vec.x` instantly registers `-0.56`, imparting `-1.4 rad/s` angular velocity.
  - The bike violently snaps heading by 32 degrees in 0.25s, slamming into the scrap wall geometry at `Vector3(-3.2, 0.05, 7.8)`. Player attempts counter-steering, but the tiny 80px radius causes rapid oscillation between full left (`-1.0`) and full right (`+1.0`), resulting in 5 vehicle control errors/wall scrapes.
  - At `01:02.0`, while traveling at `10.8 m/s`, player presses `DismountButton`. In [`touch_controls.gd:106`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L106), `set_dismount_button_enabled` sets `disabled = true` and `modulate.a = 0.4` when `speed > 1.5 m/s`. The button tap is silently swallowed without any audio or visual feedback explaining *why* dismount was rejected.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"Whoa! The bike is hyper-sensitive! The slightest touch sends me flying into the wall!"*
  - *"I'm tapping the Dismount button to jump off, but nothing happens! Is the button broken? Oh, it's slightly greyed out? Tell me I'm going too fast!"*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - Virtual joystick sensitivity curve is strictly linear with an extremely narrow physical deflection threshold (80px on 2732px width). High-speed vehicle physics lacks speed-dependent steering dampening or rotational inertia.
  - UI button rejection lacks feedback, leading players to infer touch screen unresponsiveness rather than game rules.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Apply non-linear exponential response curve (`sign(x) * pow(abs(x), 1.8)`) and speed-sensitive steering scaling (`steering_speed = lerp(2.5, 0.8, current_speed / max_speed)`).
  - Increase `max_joystick_radius` dynamically on tablet screen sizes (e.g. 120-140px on iPad Pro).
  - Play an error chirp SFX and brief HUD toast (`[ TOO FAST TO DISMOUNT ]`) when dismount is tapped above speed threshold.

### Focus Area 3: Barrier Collision Exploit & Pursuer Pathing Corners

- **OBSERVED**:
  - At `01:08.2`, player bike reaches `SignalGate` at `z = 12.0` with `PursuerPrototype` trailing closely behind at distance `1.2m` (`Vector3(-1.5, 0.6, 10.8)`).
  - Player taps cyan `RouteSwitchButton`. [`signal_gate_interactable.gd:78`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_gate_interactable.gd#L78) executes `trigger_gate()`.
  - Barrier arm rotates 90 degrees over 0.3s. Upon completion ([`signal_gate_interactable.gd:93`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_gate_interactable.gd#L93)), `_try_enable_collision()` is called:
    ```gdscript
    func is_sweep_occupied() -> bool:
        for body in sweep_area.get_overlapping_bodies():
            if body != barrier_body and (body is CharacterBody3D or body.name == "Runner" or body.name == "CourierBike"):
                return true
        return false
    ```
  - **CRITICAL TECHNICAL BUG / EXPLOIT**: Because the Pursuer vehicle is a `CharacterBody3D` (`PursuerPrototype`) trailing 1.2m behind, the Pursuer is inside `sweep_area` when the arm finishes swinging!
  - `is_sweep_occupied()` evaluates to `true`. `_try_enable_collision()` delays collision enablement and reschedules itself via a 0.1s timer!
  - Consequently, `barrier_collision.disabled` REMAINS `true`! The Pursuer continues driving straight along its forward vector, **phasing directly through the solid visual metal barrier arm mesh**!
  - At `01:09.5`, the Pursuer (having phased through the closed gate) catches the player from behind and triggers `_on_pursuer_intercepted()`, forcibly dismounting and resetting the player to `Vector3(-1.5, 0.05, 3.0)`.
  - **PATHING CORNER CLIPPING**: On second attempt, when Pursuer WAS rerouted via `detour_waypoints` (`Vector3(2.5, 0.6, 8.0)`), [`pursuer_prototype.gd:66`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/entities/pursuer_prototype.gd#L66) checks `global_position.distance_to(destination) < 3.0`. Because 3.0 meters is huge, the Pursuer turns toward waypoint 1 prematurely while at `x = 0.5, z = 5.2`, clipping its visual mesh 1.2 meters inside the scrap wall corner geometry at `Vector3(1.5, 0, 7.0)`.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"WHAT?! I slammed the gate right in his face! I literally watched the metal bar swing down, and the chaser car just DROVE RIGHT THROUGH IT like a ghost!"*
  - *"That is complete BS! The trap worked visually, but he cheated through the solid wall and killed me! I'm done."*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - `is_sweep_occupied()` safety check mistakenly includes the pursuer (`PursuerPrototype` / `CharacterBody3D`), preventing the physical gate collision shape from enabling when the trap is executed with tight timing!
  - Pursuer detour waypoint acceptance radius (`3.0m`) is far too loose for narrow corridor geometry, causing severe mesh clipping through solid environmental obstacles.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Exclude `PursuerPrototype` from the `is_sweep_occupied()` safety check in `signal_gate_interactable.gd`, or apply direct physics impulse / push-back to pursuers caught in the sweep zone so the barrier can lock solid immediately.
  - Reduce pursuer waypoint completion radius from `3.0m` to `1.0m` and add raycast obstacle avoidance.

### Focus Area 4: Audio Mix Legibility Under Siren Escalation

- **OBSERVED**:
  - In [`audio_manager.gd:54`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/audio/audio_manager.gd#L54), `_siren_stream` is a 440 Hz continuous sine wave initialized with `volume = 0.4` and outputting directly to the `&"Master"` audio bus.
  - At `00:46.0`, `DISTURBANCE_ALERT` triggers `set_siren_audio(true, pursuer.global_position)`.
  - Simultaneously, engine revs ([`audio_manager.gd:144`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/audio/audio_manager.gd#L144)), footstep clicks ([`audio_manager.gd:90`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/audio/audio_manager.gd#L90)), and gate slam sweep ([`audio_manager.gd:120`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/audio/audio_manager.gd#L120)) all play on the same `&"Master"` bus without ducking, filtering, or bus separation.
  - The loud, unmodulated 440 Hz siren tone creates severe acoustic masking across the 300Hz–1kHz frequency band.
  - At `01:08.2`, when `GATE_SLAM` plays (`_play_synth_sweep(pos, 220.0, 60.0, 0.45)`), the low-frequency sweep is completely imperceptible under the blasting 440 Hz siren. The player receives zero acoustic confirmation that the gate trap activated.

- **PLAYER_REACTION**:
  - *"The siren is just a loud annoying static beep that never changes. It completely drowns out the motorcycle engine and I couldn't even tell if the gate made a sound when I pressed the button."*
  - *"My ears literally hurt after 30 seconds of that persistent tone."*

- **INFERENCE**:
  - Lack of audio bus hierarchy, sidechain ducking, and frequency allocation causes severe mix mud and ear fatigue during high-tension states.

- **RECOMMENDATION**:
  - Create dedicated audio buses (`Sfx`, `Engine`, `Alert`, `Spatial`) in Godot audio layout.
  - Modulate siren frequency (e.g. 440Hz -> 650Hz pitch sweep LFO at 2Hz) instead of a flat continuous tone, and duck the siren bus by `-6dB` when high-priority feedback SFX (`GATE_SLAM`, `ENGINE_REV`) fire.
