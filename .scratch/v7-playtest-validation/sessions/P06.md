## 3. ADVERSARIAL FINDINGS & RETEST ANALYSIS

### 3.1 FINDING-R01.1: CRITICAL (S0) Gate Sweep Safety Check Triggered by Player Body, Causing Ghost Phase-Through

- **TAXONOMY**: Collision / Critical Game-Breaking Physics Bug
- **SEVERITY**: **S0 (Critical Failure - Game-Breaking)**
- **OBSERVED**: 
  In [`signal_gate_interactable.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/signal_gate_interactable.gd#L70-L77), commit `31681ba` attempted to prevent the pursuer from locking out barrier collision by excluding `PursuerPrototype` from `is_sweep_occupied()`. However, `is_sweep_occupied()` still checks:
  ```gdscript
  if body is PlayerRunner or body is CourierBike or body.name == "Runner" or body.name == "CourierBike":
      return true
  ```
  When the player triggers the gate while driving the `CourierBike` past `z = 12.0m` (or standing between `z = 8.8m` and `z = 15.2m`), `SweepArea` (which rotates 90° with `BarrierPivot`) overlaps the `CourierBike` or `PlayerRunner`.
  When the 0.3s swing animation finishes, `_try_enable_collision()` evaluates `is_sweep_occupied()`. Because the player's bike/runner is inside `SweepArea`, `is_sweep_occupied()` returns `true` and schedules a 0.1s delay.
  During this delay, `barrier_collision.disabled` remains `TRUE`. `PursuerPrototype` (moving at 11.0 m/s) drives straight through the solid metal barrier arm at `z = 12.0m` with zero physical collision. `barrier_collision.disabled` only becomes `false` AFTER the pursuer has already passed through the barrier to `z = 8.35m`.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"I hit the route switch button right as I drove past, watched the steel gate slam shut behind me, and the cop car drove STRAIGHT THROUGH the solid steel barrier arm like it was a ghost! The gate fix didn't work at all!"*
- **INFERENCE**: Safety sweep volume check evaluates the player's own vehicle/runner collision shape when the barrier rotates into position, causing `_try_enable_collision()` to infinitely delay enabling collision while threat entities cross the gate coordinate.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Exclude player/bike entities from `is_sweep_occupied()` once `trigger_gate()` has been initiated, or restrict sweep safety checks to a single frame instantaneous check during the initial swing phase.

### 3.2 FINDING-R01.2: Panel Peel Gesture Cancellation Leaves Mesh Frozen Mid-Air & Asymmetric Drag

- **TAXONOMY**: Gesture / Visual Art & State Disconnect
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L169), lifting the touch finger during a peel gesture emits `peel_gesture_released`. In [`scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L479), `_on_peel_gesture_released()` calls `corroded_panel.cancel_interaction()`.
  While this successfully resets `current_step` from `PEELING` to `APPROACHED` and unlocks player locomotion (`is_input_locked = false`), [`corroded_panel.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/interactions/corroded_panel.gd#L53) DOES NOT reset `panel_mesh` transforms.
  If the player releases their touch at 75px drag (`progress = 0.50`), `panel_mesh.rotation.x` remains frozen at `-37.5°` and `panel_mesh.position.z` remains frozen at `0.40m`. The metal panel stays hovering half-open in mid-air. When the player re-engages Action, `_peel_accumulated_y` resets to 0.0, but `panel_mesh.rotation.x` lerps from `-37.5°` to `-75.0°`, creating visual popping.
  Additionally, in [`touch_controls.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/input/touch_controls.gd#L187), drag parsing requires `drag_ev.relative.y > 0`. Upward drag movement (`relative.y < 0`) is completely ignored and does not decrease accumulated peel progress.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"When I let go of the touch screen halfway through peeling, the metal cover stayed floating half-open in mid-air while my character walked away. When I tapped it again, it twitched weirdly from the half-open spot. Also, dragging back up doesn't close it."*
- **INFERENCE**: `cancel_interaction()` updates logical state without resetting mesh transforms. Single-direction delta filtering (`relative.y > 0`) breaks bi-directional gesture tracking.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: In `corroded_panel.cancel_interaction()`, add a 0.2s tween resetting `panel_mesh.rotation.x` to `0.0` and `panel_mesh.position.z` to `0.0`. In `touch_controls.gd`, support bi-directional drag distance accumulation (`clampf(_peel_accumulated_y + drag_ev.relative.y, 0.0, 150.0)`).

### 3.3 FINDING-R01.3: Detour Waypoint U-Turn Teleportation & Speed Delta Bypass

- **TAXONOMY**: AI Pathing / Game Balance
- **SEVERITY**: **S2 (Major Friction)**
- **OBSERVED**:
  In [`scrap_test_block.gd`](file:///Users/bobbyinthelobby/%7Bart/godot/scripts/prototype/scrap_test_block.gd#L242), triggering the gate assigns fixed detour waypoints: `[Vector3(2.5, 0.6, 8.0), Vector3(2.5, 0.6, 18.0), Vector3(-1.5, 0.6, 26.0)]`.
  If the pursuer has driven past `z = 8.0m` (e.g. at `z = 10.5m`) when the gate button is pressed, assigning `detour_waypoints[0] = Vector3(2.5, 0.6, 8.0)` forces `PursuerPrototype` to execute an instantaneous 180-degree U-turn backwards up the track toward `z = 8.0m`.
  Furthermore, bike top speed is `14.0 m/s` while pursuer max speed is `11.0 m/s`. Simply holding GAS down the straightaway outruns the pursuer in 4.5 seconds (> 18.0m threshold), triggering `EVADED` status without requiring the SignalGate route switch.
- **PLAYER_REACTION**: *"When I hit the gate button a second late, the police car abruptly flipped 180 degrees backward and drove away from me before turning around again. Plus, I don't even need the gate because the bike is way faster than the police car."*
- **INFERENCE**: Static waypoint 0 (`z = 8.0m`) fails when pursuer Z coordinate exceeds 8.0m. Inverted speed delta trivializes pursuit evasions.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: Compute dynamic detour waypoints relative to pursuer's current position (`pursuer.global_position.z + 4.0`). Increase pursuer top speed to `16.5 m/s`.
