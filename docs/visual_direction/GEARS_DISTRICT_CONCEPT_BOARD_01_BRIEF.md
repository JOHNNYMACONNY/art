# Gears District — Concept Board 01 Brief

**Status:** `CANDIDATE_SUPPORTING_ART__NOT_CANON`  
**Parent direction:** `GEARS_DISTRICT_VISUAL_DIRECTION_V1.md`  
**Purpose:** test whether one visual language survives five different gameplay/location needs before any detailed district production planning begins.

## Render language

**CANDIDATE:** stylized graphic realism for a 3D Godot game: strong readable forms, selective cel-shaded / illustrated value grouping, materially believable wear, restrained edge accents, and crisp graphic decals. The image should feel authored and hand-designed rather than photoreal, low-poly-generic, or anime-character-centric.

Use the supplied FB-13 reference as the quality/identity anchor for surface language:

- worn off-white painted shell;
- charcoal structural mass;
- sparse cyan signal emission;
- exposed fasteners/repairs;
- stenciled numbering and asset tags;
- practical asymmetry;
- scratches and wear that follow use rather than uniform grunge.

Translate those principles to architecture and infrastructure without making the district look like a giant robot.

## Camera / presentation

- elevated 3/4 game-readable view, approximately compatible with the retained camera philosophy;
- enough height to understand streets, alleys, entrances and route options;
- avoid cinematic ground-level shots as the primary concept evidence;
- strong silhouette/value hierarchy at thumbnail size;
- concept-art board may use five linked panels or one contiguous montage, but all panels must clearly belong to the same district.

## Shared art rules

- dominant neutrals: soot charcoal, worn off-white, municipal concrete;
- localized oxidized iron and faded safety amber;
- utility teal as system coding;
- warning vermilion scarce;
- HS-7 cyan extremely scarce and meaningful;
- Echotel uses warm cream + oxblood/burgundy + warm white illumination;
- practical lights at night; no neon-everywhere cyberpunk;
- old signs/route bands ghost beneath new paint;
- large numeric IDs and clipped-corner civic plates;
- road surfaces remain calmer than walls/yards;
- one dominant landmark silhouette per panel;
- large readable signage shapes, not walls of tiny text.

## Panel A — Recovery Lot 13 / current scrapyard

Show a low salvage yard embedded in the district, not isolated in wasteland. Patched corrugated perimeter, recognizable hoist/crane silhouette, weigh/inspection structure, containers and legible salvage provenance. Integrate a tuner mast / extraction housing / gate / shortcut language as believable sub-areas. Huge `13` marker. Main drivable route remains obvious.

## Panel B — Mayor Burn's garage

Former municipal fleet-service building converted into a repair/chop/favor operation. Bent broad canopy, two dark garage bays, old official paint beneath improvised Burn branding, retired municipal vehicle body panel reused as signage. Satire should read as respectable civic language over visibly dubious activity.

## Panel C — Sister Kael / Silent Core

Former municipal relay/pump/substation: heavy quiet concrete, central exposed service core, analog cable patching arranged with ritual care, mechanically scrubbed department seal, deliberate negative space around the interactable. Warm work light. One narrow HS-7 cyan seam/response only; never a glowing cyber-temple.

## Panel D — Compliance Junction 4

Major industrial intersection used to test pursuit/navigation readability. Offset four-way crossing, overhead utility gantry framing the road, distant pipe/conveyor bridge, large `4` marker, broad main road, visually distinct service alternate and narrow shortcut. Crane/stack landmark. Route choices must read before sign text.

## Panel E — Parts & Permits Row

Dense inhabited 2–3 storey commercial strip. Repair bays, awnings, parts stores, permit/document fixer, food kiosk, salvage/pawn business, small criminal fronts that borrow official signage. One or two visible cross-block alleys. Local commerce is colorful relative to industry but still muted/material, not neon entertainment district.

## Rejection conditions

Reject/iterate if the board:

- looks like generic Blade Runner / neon cyberpunk;
- resembles GTA-specific map/art/UI/branding;
- hides routes under props or overhead structures;
- treats rust/grime as a uniform texture filter;
- gives every surface equal detail;
- makes Silent Core a fantasy shrine instead of repurposed infrastructure;
- makes the district abandoned instead of economically active;
- uses HS-7 cyan as ordinary ambient decoration;
- creates five scenes with unrelated palettes/design languages;
- cannot plausibly reduce into modular Godot architecture/props.

## What approval of the board would mean

Approval would validate the visual language, not exact building geometry or a final district map. Exact geography, modular asset breakdown, streaming/occlusion strategy and production tickets remain deferred until the parent visual direction is promoted to APPROVED.
