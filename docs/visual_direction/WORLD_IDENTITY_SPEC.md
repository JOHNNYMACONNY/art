# Gears District: World Identity & Setting Specification

## 1. Core Mandate: Separation of Camera/Shader vs Setting Content

* **"Chinatown Wars" is a technical rendering & camera reference only**:
  * 32-degree elevated gameplay projection.
  * Inverted-hull dark comic ink contour lines.
  * Two-tone graphic cel-shading with hard light/shadow breaks.
* **Content & Setting are NOT Chinatown**:
  * No literal Chinatown tropes, no decorative Chinese lanterns, no Chinese restaurant cliches.
  * No gratuitous foreign lettering or ethnic pastiche.
  * Setting is a polyglot, international near-future sci-fi semi-dystopia: the **Gears District**.

---

## 2. World Aesthetic Pillars

### A. Scrappy Resourcefulness & Kitbashed Tech
* Civil infrastructure abandoned, decaying, or strictly quota-controlled.
* Citizens and underground runners survive by stripping discarded corporate tech, heavy machinery, and industrial logistics hardware.
* High-tech gear looks cobbled together: exposed wiring conduits, military-spec surplus fasteners, welded steel brackets, modular battery cores, repurposed telemetry HUDs mounted on scavenged frames.

### B. "Clankers" (Salvaged & Repurposed Automata)
* Autonomous bots, crawlers, and droids assembled from heavy salvage parts.
* Advanced functionality (sub-millisecond sensor loops, balance gyros, high-torque servos) housed inside rusted, dented, industrial scrap chassis.
* Visible patchwork welds, mismatched plating, repurposed vehicle headlamps for optic sensors, cable bundles wrapped in electrical tape.

### C. Universal Industrial Signage & Typography
* Functional, utilitarian municipal stencil typography (DIN, Helvetica Black, Impact, monospace).
* Industrial codes, sector IDs, hazard warnings, voltage ratings, and courier routing marks:
  * `BAY-01 // COURIER DOCK`
  * `FB-13 // SCRAMBLER`
  * `HS-7 // RUNNER`
  * `SC-RELAY // DZ-03`
  * `VOLT-REC // 480V HIGH SURGE`
  * `CAUTION // PINCH POINT`
* High-visibility safety markings: diagonal hazard chevrons (caution orange / hazard amber over charcoal).

### D. Color Palette & Weathering
* **Base Architecture & Tech**: Matte charcoal (`#1A1C20`), industrial gunmetal (`#4A4F56`), weathered zinc patina, pitted cast iron, dark road asphalt.
* **Functional Accents**: High-vis signal orange (`#FF5500`), caution amber (`#E0A241`), salvage bone (`#D7D1C5`).
* **Active Cybernetics & Telemetry**: Sparse neon memory cyan (`#00E5FF`) emitted strictly from optical sensors, diagnostic displays, and battery cores.

## 3. Dan Houser Narrative DNA: Cynical Sci-Fi Satire

### A. Corporate Predation & Absurd Bureaucracy
* World run by ruthless corporate cartels disguised as civic utilities (e.g., civic quota councils, privatized air/signal syndicates).
* Dystopia mundane and commercialized: fine print, predatory loan clauses, mandatory biometric wellness subscriptions, absurd user agreements.
* Law enforcement operates like privatized debt collectors: pursuit sirens blare corporate sponsorship slogans, automated response drones charge per-bullet fees to target accounts.

### B. Street-Level Cynicism (No Chosen Saviors)
* Protagonist (HS-7) not messiah. Street courier hustler surviving between loan shark payments, hauling volatile battery cores and wiped civic archive shards across contested gang turf.
* Fixers, chop-shop mechanics (Mayor Burn), and underground couriers speak with sharp, cynical street wit. Everyone has angle, nobody works for free.

### C. Environmental & Media Satire (Radio, Storefronts, Ads)
* **In-Game Radio Stations**: Talk radio hosts parodying media narcissism, tech oligarch propaganda, fake wellness gurus, and conspiracy survivalists.
* **Storefronts & Billboards**: Biting satirical ads for synthetic stimulants, repossession-proof organs, mandatory memory backup insurance, and disposable clanker labor.
* **Clanker Personalities**: Repurposed scrap bots with glitching customer-service chips, union grievances, existential dread, or broken municipal compliance routines.

## 4. District Chromatic Architecture (GTA Signature Palettes, Anti-Cyberpunk Slop)

### Anti-Cyberpunk Guardrails
* **NO generic magenta/neon-purple wash**: Avoid dark rainy neon-drenched cliches.
* **Natural daylight & smog**: Lit by realistic daytime sun, smoggy afternoon haze, and gritty tungsten/halogen streetlights, not colored lasers.
* **Color from the environment**: Paint, weathered brick, sun-bleached tarps, oxidized copper, shipping containers, and commercial vinyl billboards provide color — not glowing light bars.

### District Palette Identities
1. **Gears District (Salvage Yard & Workshops)**:
   * *Dominant Tone*: Industrial rust, dirty soot, machinery grime.
   * *Palette*: Weathered terracotta red brick (`#8A3C2A`), pitted charcoal steel (`#1C1E22`), heavy machinery yellow (`#E5A922`), caution safety orange (`#FF5500`), galvanized zinc patina (`#8C969E`).
2. **The Yardline (Scrap Transit & Freight Rail)**:
   * *Dominant Tone*: Rail ties, scorched diesel soot, heavy logistics.
   * *Palette*: Creosote dark brown/black (`#181512`), sulfur rail amber (`#D9822B`), oxidized copper rail patina (`#3D7A68`), faded boxcar box red (`#732822`).
3. **Civic Heights / Quota Core (Administrative Hub)**:
   * *Dominant Tone*: Cold bureaucratic authority, sterile brutalism.
   * *Palette*: Precast concrete bone-white (`#D7D1C5`), slate municipal navy (`#233044`), brushed anodized aluminum (`#A4ACB8`), surveillance amber warning lights (`#FF9900`).
4. **Low-Tide Basin / Canal Slums (Flooded Underbelly)**:
   * *Dominant Tone*: Brackish water, marine decay, sun-faded plastics.
   * *Palette*: Murky algae olive-green (`#3A4839`), weathered tarp blue (`#2E658C`), bleached driftwood grey (`#9E9A90`), rusty buoy orange (`#D6542C`).
5. **Commercial Outlaw Bazaar (Night Market / Black-Market Alley)**:
   * *Dominant Tone*: Vibrant commercial chaos, analog signage.
   * *Palette*: Warm tungsten incandescent gold (`#F5BA42`), commercial Coca-Cola/service red (`#C42021`), gas-station teal (`#1C828A`), faded vinyl canopy stripes (teal & cream).
